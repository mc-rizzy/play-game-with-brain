# Hackathon guidelines (reference)

Track, theme, judging weights, and submission questions for the
Meta AI Developer Hackathon (Indie Developer Track). Our answers live in
[README.md](README.md).

## Track

Indie Developer Track: for individual builders and students. Judged on
creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works.

## Problem statement

Push the Frontier: Use Meta's models to tackle challenges in software,
robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and beyond. Give a machine a
new capability, accelerate a scientific experiment, or unlock a better way
to understand and control a physical system. Show us what becomes possible
when these models meet the real world.

## Judging

The published materials cover the criteria, not the mechanics. Judging is
weighted Impact 30%, Use of Meta AI Tooling 25%, Technical Depth and
Execution 20% and Demo 25%, and Stage One is pass/fail, checking that the
project reasonably fits a theme and reasonably uses Meta's models. Judges
work from your 3-minute demo video and your public GitHub repo, so both
should clearly show the working project and the Meta model calls.

Judging is weighted Impact 30%, Use of Meta AI Tooling 25%, Technical
Depth and Execution 20% and Demo 25%, per the Official Rules at
https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details.

Stage One is a pass/fail check that the project reasonably fits one of
the two themes and reasonably uses the Meta Model API and/or Meta's
open-weight models. Stage Two scores the projects that pass Stage One on
the rubric above. The top 5 per track then move to a final round where
judges watch the demo videos live.

Judges work from your 3-minute demo video and your public GitHub repo,
so both need to show the working project clearly.

How Stage One and Stage Two results are shared has not been announced,
and nothing published says which materials are reviewed first or whether
all demo videos are watched.

## Submission questions

- Project Description
- Describe your use of Meta products to build the submitted project
- Why are Meta's models important to your solution?
- Provide feedback from your experience using Modal.

## On Muse models

- Why was Meta's API and muse models integral for this project?
- What was the biggest obstacle your team hit while building this
  project, and how did Meta's models and API solve them?
- Compared with other AI models, how did Meta's models outperform on
  this project?
- For this project, when choosing an AI model or coding tool, what
  matters most?
- What makes it hardest for your team to switch to a different AI model
  or coding tool?
